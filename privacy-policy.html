Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Imago Construction LLC ("Imago Construction," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We collect information you choose to give us, primarily through our contact and consultation request forms. This may include your name, phone number, email address, property or project location, and any details you share about your project. We don't require account creation or payment information to use this site.

Like most websites, our hosting and analytics tools may automatically log basic technical information, such as your browser type, general location, and pages visited, to help us understand how the site is used and keep it running reliably.

How we use your information

We use the information you submit to respond to consultation requests, answer questions, schedule calls or site visits, and otherwise communicate with you about your project. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

How we share information

We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate this website or run our business (for example, email or hosting providers), and only to the extent needed for them to perform those services. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Imago Construction, our clients, or others.

Cookies and similar technologies

This site may use cookies or similar technologies to support basic functionality and to understand, in aggregate, how visitors use the site. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; doing so may affect how some parts of the site function.

Data retention

We retain the information you submit for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry, maintain our business records, and comply with legal obligations.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete the information you've submitted to us at any time by emailing ethan@imagoconstruction.com. If you've asked us not to contact you, we'll honor that request.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for adults seeking construction and home-building services and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how we handle your information, reach out at ethan@imagoconstruction.com or 865-469-3076.
