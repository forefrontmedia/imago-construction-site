Why Imago

Seven reasons homeowners choose Imago Construction.

There's no shortage of builders in East Tennessee. Here's what's actually different about building with us.

Only a few homes at a time

Ethan takes on a limited number of projects at once, on purpose. That means your calls get answered, your site visits don't compete with four other job sites, and decisions don't sit in a queue.

High performance, standard

Air sealing, a properly designed building envelope, and mechanical systems sized to the house aren't an upgrade package here — they're how every Imago home is built, whether you ask for them or not.

Real timelines, real budgets

We walk every client through a realistic phase-by-phase schedule and a budget that accounts for decisions up front, not value-engineering after the foundation's poured. Fewer surprises, fewer change orders.

Local, not a franchise

Over 20 years building in East Tennessee means we already know the counties, the permitting offices, the soil, and the sites — not just the floor plans.
