High Performance

What "high performance" really means for your home.

It's a home designed and built as a complete system, so it stays comfortable, efficient, and durable long after move-in day.

The Imago approach

Four things a high-performance home gives you.

This isn't an upgrade package or an add-on. It's how every Imago home is designed and built from the foundation up.

Lower energy costs

A tighter, better-insulated building envelope and efficient systems reduce what it costs to heat and cool.

Even comfort

Fewer drafts and fewer hot or cold spots from room to room, all year.

Healthier air

An energy recovery ventilator and a dedicated dehumidifier (not just a furnace filter) keep indoor air fresh without giving up efficiency.

Future-proofed

Wiring and infrastructure for a pool, generator, or EV charger go in during the build, so nothing has to get cut open later.

Why it matters

Built as a system, not a checklist.

Most of what makes a home comfortable and efficient for the next 30 years happens where you can't see it: in the wall assembly, the air sealing, and how the mechanical systems are sized and installed. We treat those as the main event, not an afterthought.
