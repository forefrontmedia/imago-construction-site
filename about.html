Our story

Building in East Tennessee and Oregon, hands-on with every project.

Ethan has spent more than 20 years in the construction industry, and in that time he's come to see the job a little differently than most builders do. He thinks of himself as a customer service expert first, one who happens to build houses, with the construction itself as the part that follows once that's right.

That mindset shapes how the whole team works. Imago takes on projects of every size, from full custom builds to home additions, but its focus is high-performance new construction: homes built to be efficient and comfortable for decades, not just finished on schedule. Every client gets the same level of attention, whether the job is a whole house or a single room.

  • Customer service first: you get direct answers, not a runaround
  • Cost-plus pricing, with full transparency on exactly what everything costs
  • Every home future-proofed for the pool, generator, or EV charger you haven't added yet
